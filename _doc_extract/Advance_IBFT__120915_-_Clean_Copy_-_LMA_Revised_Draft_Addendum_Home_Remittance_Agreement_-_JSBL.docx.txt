ADDENDUM 
TO THE HOME REMITTANCES INLAND DISBURSEMENT AGREEMENT
This Addendum to the Home Remittances Inland Disbursement Agreement (“Addendum”) is made on this _____ day of ________, 2015
BY AND between
JS Bank Limited, a banking company incorporated under the laws of Pakistan having its registered office at ___________________________________ (hereinafter referred to as the “Bank” which expression shall include its successors in interest and assigns) OF THE FIRST PART;
And
 <<NAME OF PARTNER EXCHANGE COMPANY>> having its registered office at ___________________ (hereinafter referred to as the “Company” which expression shall include its successors in interest and permitted assigns) OF THE SECOND PART.  
WHEREAS:
The Bank and the Company entered into a Home Remittances Inland Disbursement Agreement dated ___________ (“Agreement”) to allow the Company to utilize the Bank’s banking channels to effect home remittances in Pakistan;
The Bank and the Company now wish to include the use of IBFT Services (as defined below) via E-Channel (as defined below) to effect home remittances in Pakistan and to enable credit of proceeds into the beneficiaries’ accounts maintained with IBFT member banks within Pakistan from PKR account(s) maintained with the Bank under the Agreement.
The Company and the Bank agree that IBFT Services shall be supplied to and used by the Company under the arrangement for home remittance, as prescribed under the Agreement, subject to the terms and conditions contained in this Addendum.
NOW THEREFORE THIS ADDENDUM TO THE AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS: 
1. 	DEFINITIONS
 	“Agent(s) and Sub-Agent(s)”: Agent(s) & sub agent(s) of the Company or an authorized person(s) of an agent or sub-agent, approved by the Company to initiate IBFT transactions via E-Channel services provided by the Bank.
“Authorised Personnel”: Any of Company’s Employees, Agents, Sub-Agents and any other individual(s) authorized by the Company, who are appointed to use E-Channel pursuant to this Addendum.
	“E-Channel”: E-Channel is the Bank's electronic banking systems, which may authorize the Company and its Agents and Sub-Agents to access and use from time to time as per the terms of this Addendum.
	“Employee(s)”: An employee who works part-time or full time under a contract of the Company, whether oral or written, express or implied, and has recognized rights and duties.
“Inter Bank Funds Transfer (“IBFT”) Services”: IBFT related services supplied via an E-Channel that the Bank provides, procures or makes available to the Company from time to time to initiate funds transfer from the Company’s PKR account maintained with the Bank to an intended beneficiary’s account maintained with any of the IBFT member banks within Pakistan under the Agreement.
	“Security Procedures”: The facilities and procedures used to control the operation of the E-Channel and IBFT Services as set out in this Addendum.
	“Software”: Any software supplied by the Bank for use in conjunction with E-Channel for initiating IBFT transactions by any Authorized Personnel.
2. 	IBFT SERVICES
2.1	Subject to the Company’s compliance with the instructions and procedures set out in this Addendum, the Bank will use reasonable efforts to make the IBFT Services available to the Company. Such IBFT Services will be subject to any notifications of any restrictions received by the Bank from Authorised Personnel, including any  power of delegation exercised by such Authorised Personnel in favour of other persons who shall thereafter be Authorised Personnel, to operate the Company’s PKR account maintained with the Bank under the Agreement.
2.2	The Company shall communicate with the Bank via its Authorized Personnel. The Company shall ensure that its users only use the IBFT Services via E-Channel in accordance with all terms of this Addendum.
2.3	From time to time, the Bank may make available to the Company enhancements, improvements and upgrades to the existing IBFT Services, which shall be governed by the provisions of this Addendum.
2.4	The Company agrees that the IBFT Services via E-Channel to be performed by any Authorized Personnel will be governed by the rules, regulations and directives of the State Bank of Pakistan in force from time to time, Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947, Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002, and any applicable money laundering provisions, guidelines, directives, rules, regulations, and laws, in force from time to time.
2.5	The Bank shall have the right to alter the IBFT Services provided through its E-Channels at the sole discretion of the Bank.
3. 	INSTRUCTIONS
3.1	The Bank shall treat all apparently valid Company’s instructions, which are initiated through E-Channel for IBFT by its Employees, Agents, Sub-Agents and any other Authorized Personnel, as all such users will be created in E-Channel by the Bank and which are approved by the Company’s Authorized Personnel. The Bank shall not be responsible for any fraudulent or conflicting instructions or mandates given by the Company at any time concerning Company’s PKR account maintained with the Bank if such instructions have been received by the Bank from an Authorised Personnel. 
3.2	The Bank shall be under no obligation to verify the authenticity of the Company’s instructions or the authority of any Authorized Personnel.
3.3	The Company shall be responsible for the accuracy and completeness of IBFT instructions (including the appropriate application of the Security Procedures) and for ensuring that such instructions will achieve the Company’s intended purpose.
3.4	The Company is responsible for ensuring that the IBFT instructions are transmitted correctly. However, in the event that the Company requests the Bank to cancel any such instruction or requests for a refund from the beneficiary’s bank for whatever reason, the Bank will make all reasonable efforts to comply with the Company’s request but will not be liable for any failure to cancel or refund, if such a request is received by the Bank at a time or under circumstances that render the Bank unable to comply with the Company’s request.
3.5	The Bank may decline to act on the instruction(s), and take any action that the Bank deems appropriate in the circumstances, including cancelling rights of access and blocking access to the IBFT Services, granted to any Authorised Personnel on the request of the Company, if the Bank has reasonable grounds to believe that any applicable law, regulation or regulatory requirement makes it unlawful for the Bank to act on the given instructions or the Company’s PKR account maintained with the Bank is being used for an unlawful purpose. 
3.6	The Company hereby irrevocably acknowledges and agrees that to the extent permitted by the prevailing laws and regulations, all IBFT Services related instructions, notwithstanding that they are sent through the E-Channel, shall be legally valid, binding and enforceable against the Company and that the same shall have the same legal effect as that of written documents/ agreements. The Company further agrees not to object to the admission of any records maintained by the Bank (including but not limited to the Company’s instructions initiated by Authorized Personnel) as evidence in any legal proceedings on the basis that such records are not originals, are not in writing or are documents produced by electronic means.
4. 		CONFIDENTIALITY
4.1		The Bank may be required to share, store or transmit information about the Company, any Authorised Personnel or accounts of the Company maintained with the Bank and used for the purpose of providing the IBFT Services under the Agreement, as amended by this Addendum. Any such sharing, storage or transmission of information will be done on a confidential basis and the Bank will endeavour to maintain strict confidentiality of such information within the Bank unless: (a) otherwise required by any applicable law, regulation or request of any public or regulatory authority; or (b) where disclosure is required for the purposes of preventing crime; or (c) the Bank deems disclosure necessary to give effect to a Company’s instruction; or (d) the information was, is or becomes available to the Bank on a non-confidential basis from a person who, to the Bank's knowledge, is not bound by a confidentiality agreement with the Company or otherwise prohibited from disclosing the information to the Bank; or (e) was lawfully in the possession of the Bank before the information was disclosed to it by the Company; or (f) the parties agree in writing that the information is not confidential or may be disclosed; or (g) information which the Bank by order of a Court of competent jurisdiction, or other legal compulsion requires disclosing.
4.2		The Company must keep confidential all information about the E-Channel and the IBFT Services contained in this Addendum and all information concerning the Company’s access to and use of the E-Channels and IBFT Services. The Company may only disclose such information to the Company’s Authorized Personnel and only to the extent strictly necessary for the proper use of the E-Channels for IBFT Services. The Company shall ensure that the Authorised Personnel of the Company also maintain the confidentiality as contained in this Addendum.
4.3		The confidentiality provisions of this Addendum shall survive termination of the Agreement.
5. 		SECURITY PROVISIONS
5.1	The Company agrees to comply with the Security Procedures and any other reasonable instructions that the Bank may issue to the Company regarding the E-Channel’s security. It is the Company’s responsibility to set up, maintain and regularly review security arrangements concerning the Company’s Authorized Personnel’s access to and use of the E-Channel for IBFT Services and information stored on the Company’s computing and communications systems.
5.2	As deemed appropriate, the Company shall perform periodical reviews of the entitlements of its Authorized Personnel and their rights granted to them to access the E-Channel for IBFT Services and must notify in writing to the Bank in a timely manner in case of any change in their rights including deletion of their user IDs from the E-Channel system to avoid any unauthorized access to the E-Channel for IBFT Services.
5.3		The Company must notify the Bank immediately upon becoming aware of any actual or attempted unauthorized access to the E-Channel for IBFT Services or any unauthorized transaction or attempt to execute an unauthorized transaction pursuant to this Addendum. 
5.4		The Company must ensure that neither the Company nor its Authorized Personnel does anything which may result in the breach of security of the E-Channel, or the systems or security of any other customers of the Bank.
6. 		LIMITED WARRANTIES
The Bank will use all reasonable efforts to ensure that the E-Channel for IBFT Services will perform in substantial conformity to the description in this Addendum. To the extent permitted by law, this is the only performance warranty made by the Bank in respect to the E-Channel or the IBFT Services. The Bank shall have no liability for breach of any implied term including, without limitation, those as to satisfactory quality or fitness for any particular purpose of the E-Channel or the IBFT Services.
7. 		LIABILITY
7.1		The Company acknowledges and agrees that the Bank shall only be liable for any loss, damage or delay which the Company suffers or incurs as a direct result of the Bank's gross negligence or willful misconduct and shall not be liable for any other loss or damage of any kind.
7.2		The Bank shall not in any event be liable to the Company for any loss of business or profits or data, or indirect, consequential or special loss or damage arising out of the Company’s use of, or in connection with, the E-Channels or the IBFT Services, whether or not the Bank has been advised of the possibility of such loss or damage and whether or not arising out of negligence, breach of this Addendum or otherwise.
7.3	The Company agrees to hold the Bank indemnified and harmless from all losses and liabilities incurred by the Bank as a result of any breach by the Company of its obligations under this Addendum or any breach of the obligations of the Company as may be exercised by the Company’s Authorised Personnel; or the Bank acting on the instructions of the Company/ Authorised Personnel of the Company or other communication relating to the IBFT Services, whether or not such instruction or communication was: (a) authorized by the Company or (b) in an agreed form.
8. 	TERMINATION
8.1	Either party may terminate this Addendum in relation to an E-Channel on not less than __________(___) days' prior written notice to the other party.
8.2	The Bank shall have the right to terminate this Addendum with immediate effect in the event of a material breach on the part of the Company.
8.3	The Bank reserves the right, from time to time to suspend E-Channel services for routine, non-routine or emergency maintenance or for any other reason where the Bank reasonably considers it necessary to do so. In the event of such a suspension, the Bank will provide reasonable notice to the Company prior to the suspension.
9. 	AUTHORIZATION BY THE COMPANY
9.1	The Company authorizes and instructs the Bank to supply the E-Channel for IBFT Services in respect to the PKR account as set out for the purpose of initiating IBFT transactions under the Agreement as amended by this Addendum. 
9.2	The Company may subsequently request and authorize the Bank to provide or withdraw E-Channel for IBFT Services in respect to PKR account opened with the Bank under the Agreement as amended by this Addendum. 
10. 	USER ID CREATION
10.1	Under the terms of this Addendum, the Company shall provide ‘User ID Creation Request Form’ (the format of which shall be provided by the Bank to the Company) in respect of its Authorised Personnel. The User ID Creation Request Form should be duly signed by the Company’s Authorized Personnel who have powers to approve or nominate such users for initiating IBFT transactions through Company’s PKR account maintained with the Bank under this Addendum.
10.2	The Bank shall reserve the rights to return all such requests to the Company, which do not comply with the requirement as per the terms of this Addendum and may request the Company to provide such information in accordance with the requirements to act on the Company’s request for creation of user IDs on E-Channel for IBFT Services.
11. 	MISCELLANEOUS
11.1	Except as amended and modified by virtue of this Addendum, the terms and conditions of the Agreement shall subsist and remain valid and binding and shall be read in conjunction with the provisions of this Addendum. All other terms and conditions of the Agreement shall remain the same and except as amended and modified herein, the Addendum shall be read with and shall become an integral part of the Agreement.
