1.3 Centegy Offer 
 
1.3.1 Our offer is the licensing of ‘RTGS-Integrator’ application to one legal entity (which is JS Bank 
Limited (JSBL) operating in Pakistan) as per the agreed scope of work (refer section 1.2).  
 
1.3.2 Centegy technologies grants to the client a perpetual, personal, non-transferable and non-
exclusive right and license to use the Software, at the location(s) identified herein. The CLIENT 
represents warrants and agrees that the Software will be used only at such location(s) and 
only for the benefit of the client.  
 
1.3.3 Centegy Technologies will not be responding to any HW (Hardware) related queries as part of 
its response (i.e. not providing hardware specification and cost).  
 
1.3.4 Provision is available to embed End to End SSL encryption with the application. 
Note:  The SSL certificate acquisition, configuration and maintenance will be client 
responsibility. 
 
1.3.5 The application shall be installed and implemented at Bank’s site in Karachi, Pakistan and may 
be relocated by the Bank at any other of its branch after prior written intimation to us as 
provided in the Agreement. However, we are not responsible for the re-installation, 
configuration, training or implementation at any new location.

2 Financials 
 
2.1 Fee Quotation 
 
2.1.1 The total product fee according to the scope of work will be Rs. 4,750,000/- as per the scope 
mentioned in section 1.2. 
 
2.1.2 The fee of the assignment does not include the cost of Hardware, Operating System License 
fee, Database Engine License Fee, and Front-End Tool Software License.  
 
2.1.3 As per CENTEGY’s general policy, we do not provide source code; therefore, the fee 
mentioned above is without source code. 
 
2.1.4 The product license fee paid and related enhancements/customization will be add-up with the 
current License + Enhancement Fee for calculating maintenance fee (bugs and error fixing only) 
which will be eighteen (18%) on yearly basis in advance. 
 
2.1.5 The fee is exclusive of all local taxes/duties and would be adjustable subject to changes in the 
scope of work. Further, any future taxation or change in government policy may affect the 
current pricing.  
 
2.2 Other Fee’s 
 
2.2.1 If the Bank requires enhancement or customization or integration at any time, a ‘BRD – 
Business Requirement Document” will be initiated by Bank. In response to this CENTEGY, shall 
provide ‘Change Request – CRF’ having scope and cost estimate and will commence work as 
soon as possible after the CRF has been accepted by the client. The development charges (per 
man day) will be determined on the basis of scope of work and man days efforts required for 
any review, enhancement, customization, integration, etc. 
 
Note:  The mode of payment will be 50 percent at the time receipt of purchase order (PO) where 
as remaining 50 percent will be at the time of UAT approval (before deployment in LIVE 
environment). 
 
2.2.2 If the CLIENT requires the services of our firm to provide configuration, training and 
implementation support assistance after the specified calendar months or at other branches also 
(other than the pilot branch), then this will be charged for, on the basis of number of calendar 
days/months support required and number of branches cover respectively. A separate 
implementation fee @ Rs. 1200/- per man hour in addition, the traveling, boarding and lodging 
and other related costs will be charged on actual basis. A full day charge (8 man hours) will apply 
for all visits & stays.  
 
Note: The mode of payment will be monthly based upon number of actual hours spend during a 
calendar month.
