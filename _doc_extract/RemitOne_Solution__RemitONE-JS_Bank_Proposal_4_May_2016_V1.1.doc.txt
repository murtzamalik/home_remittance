JS Bank Limited was formed in 2006 by the merger of Jahangir Siddiqui Investment Bank (Ltd) and the commercial banking operations of American Express Bank (Pakistan) Ltd. JS Bank Limited also possesses a Primary Dealer license for government securities from the State Bank of Pakistan. With a strong focus on offering quality banking products and services to the Pakistan, JS Bank
s key activities can be categorized into four Business Units:
Retail and Consumer Banking
Treasury
Corporate and Commercial Banking
Investment Banking
JS Bank is one of the fastest growing banks in Pakistan. With a vast online network of
277 branches in 138 cities
in all provinces, we are present wherever our customers need us to be. From the biggest cities to the smallest towns, we consistently offer outstanding services along with a host of innovative and growth-oriented products.
JS Bank now wish to expand their already successful home remittance business and increase revenues using a quick to market approach. Through recent detailed discussions with RemitONE it was discovered that JS Bank could easily gain access to the RemitONE global community of send agents and banks (through RemitONE Connections) through a simple piece of integration work.
Always at the forefront of technology in the money transfer industry, RemitONE provides money transfer systems to banks, money transfer operators (MTOs), Telcos and start-ups from around the world. Our multi-agent, multi-channel and multi-currency solutions empower our clients and their customers to send and receive money using agents, EFTPOS terminals, mobile phones, prepaid cards, self-service kiosks and online web portals.
RemitONE Connections will provide JS Bank the following:
Enable JS Bank to launch a new stream of remittances fairly quickly and take advantage of RemitONE Connections (a network of banks and MTOs including a range of send and receive agents integrated with the RemitONE platform)
Integration via Web Services API to the current JS Bank remittance system. The integration will be carried out by JS Bank (at their own request) with limited support from RemitONE
JS Bank wish to receive transactions from key remittance corridors including the UK and UAE. They wish to integrate RemitONE to their current remittance system ASAP to take advantage of new revenue streams. This can be achieved potentially provided JS Bank are able to complete the contractual formalities and respond to RemitONE
s documentation and engagement procedures in a timely fashion.
JS Bank want a real time API to their current money transfer platform so they can receive transactions once the integration work is complete. It is understood that JS Bank will complete a commercial agreement with any RemitONE customer they wish to receive transactions from. This agreement will be between JS Bank and the RemitONE client and may be subject to State Bank of Pakistan approval.
Such introductions will be made once a contract for this proposal has been signed and invoices paid for by JS Bank.
RemitONE proposes to satisfy JS Bank
s requirements through integration to the JS Bank remittance system, to deliver RemitONE Connections so that JS Bank can connect potentially with any RemitONE client to receive transactions. JS Bank have chosen to carry out the integration work themselves with support from RemitONE.

The Value Add
JS Bank can immediately access other money service businesses from send and payout countries that are on the RemitONE platform. JS Bank will save significant time and costs and we can have them setup and receiving transactions quickly.
The idea behind RemitONE Connections is simple and fundamental to the money transfer industry. We give JS Bank the opportunity to rapidly and easily increase their monthly transaction volumes by bringing our coverage and network to them. The more transactions JS Bank receives, the more revenue and enhanced customer loyalty for their business.
How Does It Work?
Becoming connected to RemitONE Connections is simple. Integration of JS Bank
s current remittance system to RemitONE gives them access to our impressive list of clients from send and payout markets across 100 countries. We will introduce JS Bank to the senior staff of any RemitONE client or partner in the send or payout countries they are interested in once a contract has been signed and invoices have been paid. Our introduction will usually be done via email or phone with a personal recommendation, thus increasing JS Bank
s chances of establishing a business relationship.
We have made the RemitONE Connections on-boarding process very easy.
JS Bank will simply integrate their current system to RemitONE, to join the RemitONE Connections network and pay monthly transaction fees which are dependent on the number of transactions they receive through our money transfer platform. There will be a minimum monthly transaction fee.
